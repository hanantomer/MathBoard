Site: themathboard.com — live classroom lessons and guest AI-tutor practice on the same board.

You already have a Google Ads conversion ID (AW-18006829563) and GA4 (G-5TXR2KXWS2). The gap is that Ads is not actually measuring conversions yet: index.html only loads GA4, and nothing fires a conversion event on sign-up or practice start. Fix that before spending.

Google Ads is paid traffic (Search, YouTube, Display). That is different from AdSense (ads on the page). Use Ads to buy visits; do not put AdSense on the board itself.

Audience and policy

Practice is used by students, including minors. Do not target under-18, do not say “for kids,” and turn off remarketing lists that could include children. Bid on teachers, parents, homeschoolers, and college students.

Tracking (do this first)

  1. In Google Ads, link the Ads account to GA4 G-5TXR2KXWS2, and turn on auto-tagging.
  2. Load the Ads tag next to GA4 (gtag('config', 'AW-18006829563')), not only the ID in vue-gtag.
  3. Create 3 conversions in Ads, then fire them from the app:
    • Start practice (guest opens /practice or /practice/blank) — volume, good for Smart Bidding
    • Teacher sign-up — highest value
    • Student sign-up — secondary
  4. Confirm with Google Tag Assistant: click the CTA, see the conversion hit. Until that works, Smart Bidding has nothing to learn.

Until you have paid plans, give those conversions dummy values (for example practice = 1, teacher = 10) so bidding has a signal.

Campaigns to start (Search only)

Keep classroom and practice separate. Match the ad to the landing page.

Campaign Intent Land on
Practice / AI tutor “AI math tutor”, “online math whiteboard practice”, “check my math work” /practice or /practice/blank (no account)
Teachers “math whiteboard for teachers”, “collaborative math board”, “virtual math classroom” / or /registerTeacher
Brand “themathboard”, “math whiteboard” /

Skip generic “math” / “calculator” — expensive and the wrong people. Add negatives: jobs, salary, PDF worksheets, cheat, answer key, kids games.

Budget and bidding

  • Start with manual CPC or Maximize clicks with a low cap until you have about 30 conversions in 30 days.
  • Then switch to Maximize conversions on the practice campaign (it will convert first).
  • Prefer desktop/tablet bid adjustments; the board is weak as a phone-first product.
  • Stay off Performance Max / Display until Search conversions are stable.

Sitelink assets (Google Ads)

Sitelinks are extra links under a Search ad. Create them in Google Ads: Campaigns → Assets → + → Sitelink. Add them at account level, use Create new, and save at least these six (unique URLs; both description lines filled).

Landing pages

  • Practice ads must promise check-answer / AI tips and send people to /practice, not teacher signup.
  • Classroom ads should say live lesson + students, and send people to /.
  • Keep HTTPS, fast load, and the first CTA visible without a login wall for practice.
Do not do yet. YouTube/Display, remarketing of students, optimizing for “page view.” Those burn budget and can conflict with child-safety rules.
